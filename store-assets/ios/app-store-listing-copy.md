## Description (max 4000 chars)
```
KABA is Algeria's trusted marketplace — buy and sell locally, or order products from abroad and have them delivered to your door.

BUY & SELL ACROSS ALGERIA
• Browse thousands of listings: electronics, fashion, vehicles, home & garden, and more
• Post your own listing in minutes with photos, price, and location
• Chat with buyers and sellers directly in the app — or on WhatsApp
• Find deals near you: Algiers, Oran, Constantine, Annaba, and all 58 wilayas

SHOP FROM ABROAD (MICRO-IMPORTATION)
Can't find it in Algeria? KABA connects you with verified Algerian travelers who bring products back from France, the UK, Germany, the UAE and more.
• Search international stores directly in the app — prices shown in DZD
• Compare verified travelers by price and delivery date
• Track your order every step: confirmed → purchased → in transit → delivered

SECURE PAYMENTS & ESCROW PROTECTION
Your money is protected. Pay a small deposit through the app (CIB / Edahabia card or BaridiMob) and the rest cash on delivery.
• KABA holds the deposit until you confirm delivery — the seller only gets paid when you receive your item
• Choose deposit-only (25% now, rest on delivery) or pay in full
• Delivery by trusted couriers with tracking numbers
• Dispute resolution if anything goes wrong

BUILT-IN MESSAGING
• In-app chat with photo sharing and product cards
• Push notifications for new messages, orders, and deliveries

TRUSTED COMMUNITY
• Verified sellers with ratings and reviews
• Seller profiles with full listing history

Whether you're selling your old phone, hunting for a deal in your city, or ordering something special from abroad — KABA makes it simple, safe, and 100% Algerian.

سوق كابا — بيع واشترِ في الجزائر، أو اطلب منتجاتك من الخارج مع توصيل مضمون ودفع آمن.
```
